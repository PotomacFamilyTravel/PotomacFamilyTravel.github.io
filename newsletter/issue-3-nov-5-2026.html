Issue #3: Free 5th Night at Moon Palace Punta Cana & Sandals/Beaches Black Friday

The Potomac Family Travel & 7-7-7 Weekly BriefThursday edition • November 5, 2026By Patrick, Fora Certified Travel Advisor

Black Friday season has officially started for Caribbean and Mexico resorts, and two of this week's offers are worth a look for winter and spring travel. As always, offers are listed only when verified in our inbox; everything else is labeled typical advisor perks, vary by date.

🌴 Moon Palace: Free 5th Night in Punta Cana (Book by Nov 18)

Palace Resorts extended its Punta Cana offer. Book 5 or more nights at Moon Palace The Grand – Punta Cana and your family gets a complimentary 5th night, plus $250 in resort credit, airport transfers and complimentary stays for kids & teens. Book now through November 18, 2026 for stays through January 31, 2027.
