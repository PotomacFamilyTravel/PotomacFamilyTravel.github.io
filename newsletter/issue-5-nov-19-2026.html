Issue #5: Sandals/Beaches 7-7-7 Refresh & Atlantis Early Access Cyber Sale

The Potomac Family Travel & 7-7-7 Weekly BriefThursday edition • November 19, 2026By Patrick, Fora Certified Travel Advisor

Black Friday week brings a fresh Sandals & Beaches 7-7-7 and an Atlantis early-access sale. As always, offers are listed only when verified in our inbox; everything else is labeled typical advisor perks, vary by date.

🏝️ Sandals & Beaches: New 7-7-7 (Book Nov 18 – 24)

A new weekly 7-7-7 list is out: an additional 7% off on select suites, 3-night minimum, for bookings made November 18 – 24, 2026. It is combinable with standard resort offers and loyalty/travel discounts, so it can stack with the Black Friday Unlocked sale (bookings through December 1: up to 65% off plus resort credits and up to $750 air credit). Eligible categories include:

  • Beaches Turks & Caicos: Treasure Beach Concierge Suite, Italian Penthouse Oceanview Concierge Family Suite w/ Kids Room, Seaside One Bedroom Concierge Suite
  • Beaches Negril: Oversized Concierge Family Suite, Grand Luxe Family Rooms, Oceanview Concierge Family Rooms (2 connecting bedrooms), 3-Bedroom Walkout Grand Butler Family Suite
  • Sandals: select suites at Ocho Rios, Caribbean Cay, South Coast, Nassau, Curaçao, St. Lucia La Toc and Montego Bay

Travel windows differ by suite (Beaches Turks & Caicos has dates from November 27, 2026 into 2028), so reply with your dates and we'll check whether they qualify. Sandals Jamaica South Coast also reopened on November 18. Special rates can change or be withdrawn at any time. How 7-7-7 works →

🌊 Atlantis: Early Access Cyber Sale (Book Before Nov 22)

Stay four or more nights for up to 30% off with up to $900 in resort credit, plus extra perks that disappear once the sale opens to the public. Book before November 22 to get them. The Cove has its own offer: up to 20% off and up to $1,000 resort credit on 4+ night stays. Terms apply. Reef vs Royal vs Cove →

🏨 Baha Mar: Hyatt Privé Offer, Book by Dec 8

No new bulletin this week. The Hyatt Privé offer is still open: up to 25% off for World of Hyatt members (up to 20% for non-members) with Privé benefits, for stays through April 30, 2027. Booking closes December 8, 2026. Grand Hyatt vs Rosewood vs SLS →
