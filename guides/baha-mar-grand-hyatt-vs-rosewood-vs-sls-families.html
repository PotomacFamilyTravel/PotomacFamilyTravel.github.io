Baha Mar for Families: Grand Hyatt vs Rosewood vs SLS

By Patrick • Fora Certified Travel AdvisorUpdated: October 23, 2026

Perks described as typical are general advisor-program perks and vary by date, rate and availability. Supplier offers are only quoted when verified and current.

One campus, three very different hotels

Baha Mar sits on Cable Beach, about 15 minutes from Nassau's airport. Its three hotels share the beach, the Baha Bay water park (separate admission/packages may apply) and many restaurants — but they feel very different.

Grand Hyatt Baha Mar — the family default

The largest of the three, with the widest room mix, many pools and the easiest access to Baha Bay. For most families with kids, this is the best fit and best value. Grand Hyatt bookings can earn World of Hyatt points.

Rosewood Baha Mar — quiet luxury for multi-gen trips

Smaller and more refined, with spacious suites and a calmer atmosphere. Great for grandparents-included trips or families with older kids who want a residential feel.

SLS Baha Mar — parents' getaway

Stylish, social and adult-leaning. Families can stay, but SLS shines for couples or parents' long weekends.

Typical advisor perks

Grand Hyatt bookings through Hyatt Privé and Rosewood bookings through Rosewood Elite typically add amenities such as daily breakfast, a property credit and upgrades/early check-in when available. These are typical advisor perks and vary by date. We have no current Baha Mar supplier promotion to advertise this week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Baha Mar hotel is best for kids?

Grand Hyatt Baha Mar is usually the best family fit: the largest room selection, many pools and easy access to the Baha Bay water park.

Is Rosewood Baha Mar good for families?

Yes, especially multi-generational families wanting a quieter, more luxurious stay with larger suites.

What do Hyatt Privé or Rosewood Elite add?

Typically breakfast, a property credit and upgrades or flexible check-in/out when available; benefits vary by date and rate.
