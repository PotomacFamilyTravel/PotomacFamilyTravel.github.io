Atlantis Reef vs Royal vs Cove: Which Tower Is Best for Your Family?

By Patrick • Fora Certified Travel AdvisorUpdated: October 23, 2026

Perks described as typical are general advisor-program perks and vary by date, rate and availability. Supplier offers are only quoted when verified and current.

The quick answer

  • The Reef — condo-style suites, many with kitchenettes; best for families who want space and the option to make breakfast or snacks.
  • The Royal — the iconic central towers; shortest walks to Aquaventure, the casino-level dining and the marine habitats. Usually the best value entry point.
  • The Cove — all-suite, more upscale and quieter, with an adults-only pool area. Atlantis is reimagining every Cove ocean suite, shared space and pool, with a new Beach Club planned for 2027 — ask us about renovation timing for your dates.

Matching a tower to your kids' ages

Toddlers & young kids: The Reef (kitchenette, space for naps) or The Royal (short walks). Tweens & teens: The Royal for independence near the action. Multi-generational groups: The Reef's larger suites or connecting rooms; The Cove for grandparents who want quieter luxury.

Everyone gets Aquaventure

Resort guests at any of the three towers get Aquaventure water park access, so the choice is mostly about room style, location and budget.

What an advisor can add

Where Atlantis participates in advisor programs (such as Virtuoso or Fora Reserve) for your room type and dates, typical amenities can include breakfast, a property credit and upgrades when available. These vary by date; we only quote Atlantis promotions that are current.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Atlantis tower is best for families with young kids?

The Reef is often best for young kids because many suites have kitchenettes and more space; The Royal is the most central and usually the best value.

Is The Cove at Atlantis being renovated?

Yes. Atlantis announced it is reimagining every ocean suite, shared space and pool at The Cove, with a new Beach Club planned for 2027.

Do all Atlantis towers include Aquaventure?

Resort guests staying at The Reef, The Royal or The Cove have Aquaventure access included.
