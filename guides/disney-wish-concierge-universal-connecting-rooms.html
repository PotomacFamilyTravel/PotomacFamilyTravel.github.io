A secondary service

Our focus is Turks & Caicos, Baha Mar, Atlantis and Moon Palace, but we also plan Disney cruises and Universal Orlando stays.

Concierge staterooms

Disney Cruise Line's Concierge level adds a dedicated lounge and team and earlier booking windows for activities. Concierge sells out early, so book as soon as itineraries open.

Connecting rooms at Universal

Book room types that can connect, request it at booking, and follow up with the hotel before arrival. Connecting rooms are a request, not a guarantee, unless the hotel confirms in writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer a Disney Cruise booking to an advisor?

Disney Cruise Line generally allows transferring a direct booking to an advisor shortly after deposit; ask us about your booking.

Are connecting rooms guaranteed at Universal hotels?

Generally they're requests; we follow up with the hotel to improve the chances.
