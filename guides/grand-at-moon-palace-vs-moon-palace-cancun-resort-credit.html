The Grand at Moon Palace vs Moon Palace Cancún — and How Resort Credit Really Works

By Patrick • Fora Certified Travel AdvisorUpdated: October 23, 2026

Perks described as typical are general advisor-program perks and vary by date, rate and availability. Supplier offers are only quoted when verified and current.

Two resorts, one huge complex

Moon Palace Cancún and The Grand at Moon Palace sit side by side south of Cancún airport. Both are family-friendly all-inclusives with kids' clubs and water play; The Grand is the newer, more upscale section with larger suites, while Moon Palace Cancún is typically the better value.

Which to pick

  • Choose The Grand for more space, newer rooms and a more polished feel — great for multi-gen groups.
  • Choose Moon Palace Cancún if value matters most and you want the classic Moon Palace experience.
  • Moon Palace Punta Cana is the Dominican Republic option for families flying into PUJ.

Resort credit, explained

Palace offers often include a resort credit — a set amount per booking that can be applied to eligible extras such as spa services, excursions, golf or upgraded dining. It is not cash and not a discount on the room rate, and each service usually has its own credit-redemption rules. We help you plan which services to use it on before you arrive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Grand at Moon Palace worth it over Moon Palace Cancún?

For families wanting bigger, newer suites and a more upscale feel, usually yes; Moon Palace Cancún is the better value pick.

How does Moon Palace resort credit work?

Resort credit is applied to eligible extras like spa, excursions, golf or special dining under each service's rules. It is not cash and does not reduce the room rate.
