Beaches Turks & Caicos 7-7-7 Promo: How It Works & Transferring Your Booking

By Patrick • Fora Certified Travel AdvisorUpdated: October 23, 2026

Perks described as typical are general advisor-program perks and vary by date, rate and availability. Supplier offers are only quoted when verified and current.

What is the 7-7-7 promo?

Each Wednesday, Sandals & Beaches release a rotating list of room and suite categories with an extra 7% off for specific travel windows, bookable for about a week. The list changes every week, so a suite that isn't on it today may appear later.

How we use it for you

  • We note your suite category and travel dates.
  • Each Wednesday we compare them against the new list.
  • If your suite appears and your booking is still eligible, we ask about applying the offer.

Transferring a direct or on-site booking

If you booked directly or at the on-property Loyalty Desk and haven't paid in full, the booking can often be transferred to an advisor. You typically keep your suite and confirmation number. Eligibility and rules are set by Sandals/Beaches and can change.

Best family suites at Beaches TCI

Butler and concierge family suites with separate kids' rooms are the most popular; two-bedroom options suit families of 5–6 or grandparents traveling along.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often does the Beaches 7-7-7 list change?

Every week — a new list is released on Wednesdays.

Can I transfer a Beaches booking made at the resort's Loyalty Desk?

Often yes, before final payment; eligibility depends on Sandals/Beaches rules.

Will I lose my booking details if I transfer?

Typically your suite and confirmation number stay the same.
