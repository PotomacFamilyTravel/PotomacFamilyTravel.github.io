TURKS & CAICOS

Beaches Turks & Caicos & Grace Bay Family Resorts

Why families love it: calm, shallow Grace Bay water, short nonstops from the East Coast, and at Beaches a kids' camp, water park and transfers included.

Best room types: Beaches butler & concierge family suites with separate kids' rooms; multi-bedroom condo-suites and villas at other Grace Bay resorts.

Advisor perks: weekly 7-7-7 list checks against your suite; at other TCI luxury hotels, typical program perks (breakfast, credit, upgrades when available).

BAHA MAR

Grand Hyatt, Rosewood & SLS Baha Mar

Why families love it: three hotels on one Cable Beach campus, the Baha Bay water park, and 15 minutes from Nassau airport.

Best room types: Grand Hyatt family rooms & suites (best all-round family pick); Rosewood suites for multi-gen luxury; SLS for parents-only escapes.

Advisor perks: typical Hyatt Privé (Grand Hyatt) and Rosewood Elite (Rosewood) amenities — breakfast, property credit, upgrades when available. Vary by date.

Check Baha Mar in the calculator →
ATLANTIS

Atlantis Paradise Island — The Reef, The Royal & The Cove

Why families love it: Aquaventure water park, marine habitats and kids' programs, all included with a resort stay.

Best room types: The Reef for kitchenettes & space; The Royal for central location; The Cove for all-suite upscale stays (reimagining underway, new Beach Club 2027).

Advisor perks: typical program amenities where eligible (e.g., Virtuoso / Fora Reserve), plus tower and renovation-timing advice.

Check Atlantis in the calculator →
MOON PALACE

Moon Palace Cancún, The Grand & Moon Palace Punta Cana

Why families love it: huge all-inclusive with water park, kids' club and frequent resort-credit offers.

Best room types: The Grand at Moon Palace for larger suites; Moon Palace Cancún for value; Punta Cana for a DR option.

Advisor perks: Palace PRO offers when current (the last verified offer — up to 45% off, $500 resort credit, transfers, kids & teens free — closed Sep 29, 2026). We'll quote what's live.

Which Atlantis tower is best for families?

The Reef for space and kitchenettes, The Royal for a central location, The Cove for upscale all-suite stays (renovation underway; new Beach Club planned for 2027).

Grand Hyatt, Rosewood or SLS at Baha Mar?

Grand Hyatt is usually the best family fit; Rosewood suits multi-gen luxury; SLS is adult-leaning.

Can I transfer an existing Beaches Turks & Caicos booking?

Often yes, if it isn't paid in full. You typically keep your suite and confirmation, and we watch each Wednesday 7-7-7 list.

Does booking through a Fora advisor cost extra?

No agency fee on standard resort bookings — we're paid by the supplier. Perks listed as typical vary by date and availability.
